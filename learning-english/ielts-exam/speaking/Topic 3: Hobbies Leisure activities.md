### Important

My study notes may contain old or unnatural English.

Do NOT blindly copy them.

If an expression sounds unnatural, too formal, or memorised, tell me and replace it with something a native British speaker would naturally say.

For example:

❌ "I really get into the feeling that I can float as free as a fish."

More natural:

✅ "I really enjoy the feeling of being able to float freely in the water."

Also pay attention to expressions such as:

* "take it easy"
* "let off steam"
* "unwind"
* "idle away my hours"
* "a wide range of interests"
* "take up a hobby"
* "try doing something"
* "try to do something"

Explain how these expressions are naturally used when necessary.

### Important grammar distinction

Teach me the difference between:

**try to do something** = make an effort to do something

Example:

> "I tried to learn how to swim."

**try doing something** = experiment with an activity to see what happens

Example:

> "I tried swimming and really enjoyed it."
